TS_CF_THERMAL_STEAM / TS_CF_THERMAL_STEAM_2030 (2026-10-23)
Hourly availability (capacity factor) profile for the STEAM thermal technology class, bound to the technologies by
Par_TagTechnologyToTimeseries (scenario overlays). The base CSVs are 1 everywhere (no effect); the
NorthAmerica_climate_<central|extreme|severe> overlays of the _2030 folder hold the SSP2-4.5 heat / cooling-water
stress derates (NA_inputs/SU Climate/GENeSYS_CapacityFactor_thermal_stress_<sens>.csv: nine US regions, summer
window hours 3622-6478, identical for every year 2030-2050, hence one 2030 variant; 1 outside the
window and for regions without data). central = temperature only; extreme = temperature + river temperature /
flow; severe = extreme with the historic drought years baked in. Members: CHP_Biomass_Solid, CHP_Biomass_Solid_CCS, CHP_Coal_Hardcoal, CHP_Coal_Hardcoal_CCS, CHP_Coal_Lignite, CHP_Coal_Lignite_CCS, CHP_Oil, CHP_WasteToEnergy, P_Biomass, P_Biomass_CCS, P_Coal_Hardcoal, P_Coal_Hardcoal_CCS, P_Coal_Lignite, P_Coal_Lignite_CCS, P_Gas_Steam, P_Nuclear, P_Nuclear_SMR, P_Oil.

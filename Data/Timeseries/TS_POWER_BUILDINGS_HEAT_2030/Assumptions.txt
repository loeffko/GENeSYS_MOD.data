TS_POWER_BUILDINGS_HEAT_<YYYY> / TS_POWER_BUILDINGS_COOLING_<YYYY> (2026-10-23)
Year-banded variants of the regular heating / cooling profiles: the model uses the regular profile for
years before 2030, the _2030 sheet for 2030-2034 and the _2035 sheet from 2035 on (latest year <= model year).
Source: SSP2-4.5 hourly region-mean temperature for the nine US ISO regions (NA_inputs/SU Climate/
2026-10-14 - Climate projections SSP2-45 for US.xlsx, UTC like the 2018 series). Non-US columns copy the
regular profile (no projection available), so the variant is a no-op there.
Method (GENeSYSMOD.tools GIS_&_Timeseries_Tool/functions.py::temperature_timeseries): heating = max(20 - T, 0)
normalised per region to (x / mean + 0.25) / 1.25; cooling = max(T - 22, 0). The tool evaluates the formula per
grid point and averages afterwards; the projections only give the region mean, and the formula on the mean
sharpens the profile (cooling peak/mean +40-75 %). The sub-regional spread is therefore restored as
E[max(X - a, 0)] with X ~ N(T_mean, sigma_r); sigma_r (heating and cooling separately, per region) is fitted so
that the 2018 region-mean temperature reproduces the point-based 2018 profile of the repository
(NA_inputs/SU Climate/derived/spread_calibration_2018.csv; shape correlation 0.98-1.00, peaks within a few %).
Generated by NA_inputs/build_climate_scenarios.py.

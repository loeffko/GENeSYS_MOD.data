TS_POWER_GENERAL (2026-10-23) - residual load shape for the Power_General end use.
TS_LOAD is the measured pool load and already contains the electric heating and cooling load. When
Power_Buildings_Heat and Power_Buildings_Cooling carry their own temperature-derived profiles on top of the
load shape, both peaks are counted twice (v6 base: winter-peaking in 7 of 9 US pools; cooling split: summer
peaks 6-42 % above the 2024 ISO peaks). This profile is (general + heat + cooling energy of the base year) x
load shape - heat x heating shape - cooling x cooling shape, clipped at zero (a few hours per pool) and
normalised to its maximum, so that general + heat + cooling reproduce the measured load shape in the base
year; later years inherit the shape. Regions without the end-use split keep the load shape. Bound to
Power_General by Par_TagFuelToTimeseries (NorthAmerica_climate_* overlays). Check table:
NA_inputs/SU Climate/derived/residual_general_shape.csv. Generated by NA_inputs/build_climate_scenarios.py.

TS_CF_THERMAL_OCGT / TS_CF_THERMAL_OCGT_2030 (2026-10-23)
Hourly availability (capacity factor) profile for the OCGT thermal technology class, bound to the technologies by
Par_TagTechnologyToTimeseries (scenario overlays). The base CSVs are 1 everywhere (no effect); the
NorthAmerica_climate_<central|extreme|severe> overlays of the _2030 folder hold the SSP2-4.5 heat / cooling-water
stress derates (NA_inputs/SU Climate/GENeSYS_CapacityFactor_thermal_stress_<sens>.csv: nine US regions, summer
window hours 3622-6478, identical for every year 2030-2050, hence one 2030 variant; 1 outside the
window and for regions without data). central = temperature only; extreme = temperature + river temperature /
flow; severe = extreme with the historic drought years baked in. Members: P_Gas_OCGT, P_H2_OCGT.
Generated by NA_inputs/build_climate_scenarios.py.

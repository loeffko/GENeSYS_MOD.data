
NorthAmerica_nuclear_announced scenario (2026-08-02):
	Nuclear corridor (min AND max) replaced by the full announced-projects
	pipeline from the two SNL-based reviews in NA_inputs/ ("Nuclear Review w
	Distributed Breakout_5.7.2026.xlsx", "Canadian Nuclear Review_5.7.2026.xlsx"),
	assuming every announced/planned/IRP/RFP unit, restart and uprate arrives
	ON TIME. Built on the v6-freeze data state for comparability with the v6
	base case.
	Method:
	- Anchor = v6 2025 min corridor (= operating fleet).
	- Additions dated by 'Scenario In Service Date' year; units with dates
	  <= 2025 treated as already in the operating fleet (excludes the Bruce
	  CDU 3/4 rows carrying their 1978/79 original in-service dates).
	- Unit class split: <= 500 MW/unit -> P_Nuclear_SMR (BWRX-300, AP300,
	  Natrium, Entra1, Cascade, TVA/Holtec SMR fleet, Oklo micro-fleets ...);
	  > 500 MW -> P_Nuclear. Restarts (Palisades, Crane, Duane Arnold) and
	  uprates (existing plants) -> P_Nuclear. Canadian uprates use the
	  per-unit uprate DELTA (75 MW Bruce units), not nameplate.
	- Refurbishment outages: where the v6 min dips below the 2025 fleet
	  (Canada 2026-2034, Bruce/Darlington refurb), the outage is kept in the
	  scenario path - those units are offline, not new builds.
	- Max corridor = max(v6 max, announced path + 0.001) so the forced path
	  is always feasible; where v6 assumed MORE capacity than the pipeline
	  supports (ERCOT +3.2 GW, SPP +2.4 GW, some SERC by 2040), the
	  scenario REPLACES the corridor with the announced path (scenario
	  question is "what the pipeline delivers", not "pipeline + old
	  assumptions").
	- Levy County units excluded (Postponed/Cancelled). Units with in-service
	  2041+ excluded (model horizon 2040): Comanche Peak 4, Blue Castle 2,
	  Turkey Point 7, Stewart County, Vogtle 5, Portland General IRP wave -
	  ~10 GW sits just past the horizon.
	Capex/params deliberately unchanged: capacity is forced via the corridor,
	so cost assumptions only affect the objective value, not the buildout.

NorthAmerica_nuclear_announced_ccgtrelax variant (2026-08-02):
	Same nuclear corridor as NorthAmerica_nuclear_announced, plus the
	P_Gas_CCGT min corridor lowered per region-year by the amount the
	nuclear min corridor increased (floored at 0). Tests whether the
	announced nuclear displaces the FORCED gas build once the funnel lower
	bound gives it room, instead of crowding out economic renewables.
	Gas max corridor and the (non-retirable) residual gas fleet untouched.

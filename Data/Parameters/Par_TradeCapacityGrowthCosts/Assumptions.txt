Par_TradeCapacityGrowthCosts — assumptions (overhauled 2026-07-30; supersedes the previous DeSantis-2021-based note: Table 1 $/mile-MW -> EUR2021 at 0.9 utilization)

Structure: per-pair values KEPT (no single per-fuel factor) because land vs
subsea drives 2-4x cost differences. Classification of every route pair into
LAND / SUBSEA / MIXED from Par_TradeRoute geography (27 subsea pairs incl.
UK/IE/NO/Baltic crossings and the DE offshore-hub pairs; 4 mixed pairs
DE-DK, FI-SE, IE-UK, ES-FR with per-pair values; all NA pairs = land).

CAPACITY BASIS (user decision 2026-07-30): values are per GW of USABLE net
transfer capacity (NTC). AC-thermal source costs are divided by a 60%
NTC-of-thermal baseline; HVDC ratings and EHB pipeline capacities (75%-derated
design capacity) are treated as already usable.

Power (M€/GW/km, EUR ~2024):
	- EU land 0.9: ACER UIC 2026 400kV AC 2-ckt (avg 1.46 M€/km at ~3 GVA
	  thermal -> 0.49 thermal-GW -> /0.6 = 0.81 NTC) blended with land-HVDC
	  all-in incl. converters 0.9-1.3 (MISO MTEP24, Haertel et al. 2017;
	  DeSantis 2021 converter-inclusive 0.86 consistent).
	- EU subsea 1.7: realized 1.4-2 GW HVDC links all-in 1.7-2.8 (NordLink,
	  Viking Link, NeuConnect); ACER 2026 submarine cable (avg 1.31 M€/km)
	  + converter pair (0.42 M€/MW) folded at 300-700 km link lengths.
	- Mixed pairs: DE-DK 1.4 (Jutland land + East-DK subsea), FI-SE 1.4
	  (north land border, main capacity subsea), IE-UK 1.7 (GB links
	  subsea), ES-FR 1.1 (Pyrenees AC + Biscay subsea share). Judgment.
	- NA land 1.5: MISO MTEP24 AC 0.88-1.03 M$/GVA/km thermal (incl. 30%
	  contingency + 7.5% AFUDC) /0.6 NTC = 1.47-1.72, blended with
	  long-corridor HVDC all-in 1.2-1.7. REPLACES the Saadi et al. 2018
	  0.445 line-only basis, which excluded converter stations (stated in
	  DeSantis 2021) - a data-correctness fix that ~triples NA IC expansion
	  cost vs. v6.
	- Previous DeSantis 0.864 rows decoded: US 500kV land-HVDC 3 GW incl.
	  converters, loss-adjusted (Black & Veatch/WECC basis) - superseded by
	  the class values above.

Gas_Natural (M€/(PJ/yr)/km; model gas trade capacity is PJ-denominated,
1 GW = 31.536 PJ/yr):
	- Onshore 0.003: ACER UIC 2015 large-diameter EU actuals (~0.0027 at
	  36-48", 17-28 GW) + escalation; recent actuals bound above at
	  0.005-0.009 (OGJ/EPA 2019, ACER 2026 H2-ready 36-48"). Replaces
	  0.0018 (= DeSantis/Rui 1992-2008 US regression, cheapest equation in
	  the literature per NETL 2024 ordering).
	- Subsea 0.006: EHB offshore/onshore factor 1.7x; Nord Stream realized
	  0.0031 as giant-scale lower bound (48" twin, no midline compression).

H2 (M€/(PJ/yr)/km):
	- Onshore new 0.008: EHB 2022 large/medium new-build incl. compression
	  (48" 0.0083, 36" 0.0170; capacities are 75%-derated = usable). The
	  previous 0.0049 cited DeSantis but does not reproduce it (true
	  DeSantis 36" = 0.0031; 0.0049 back-solves to an undocumented ~1.6x).
	- Subsea new 0.018: EHB 2022 offshore new incl. compression
	  (medium 0.0286, large 0.0143). ES-FR classified subsea for H2
	  (H2Med/BarMar is explicitly subsea).
	- Repurposed pipelines (0.0027-0.0048 incl. compression, EHB) are NOT
	  modelled as a separate class - flagged as a large EU-corridor lever.

Biomass (M€/(PJ/yr)/km): 0.0039 kept as nominal placeholder - no citable
per-km capacity capex exists (terminals/rolling stock are mostly
distance-independent; a rolling-stock estimate gives 0.001-0.002). The real
logistics cost is OPEX and lives in Par_TradeCostFactor (DEA freight).

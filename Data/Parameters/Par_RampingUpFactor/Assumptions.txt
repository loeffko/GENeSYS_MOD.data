Par_RampingUpFactor — assumptions (power side 2026-06-02, heat/industry side 2026-07-30)

Convention:
	- Value = fraction of available capacity the technology can move per hour.
	- Literature ramp rates are HALVED to compensate the perfect-foresight
	  optimism of the linear model (applies to all nonzero entries).
	- 0 = constraint deliberately DISABLED (unlimited ramping) - used for
	  technologies that traverse their full range well within an hour.
	- Up- and down-factors are kept symmetric (no source distinguished them).

Power plants (2026-06-02): real-world fleet ramping from Brouwer et al. 2015,
EPRI, IRENA, DEA technology data; halved. Coal 0.45, lignite 0.30, CCGT 0.60,
gas steam 0.50, OCGT/engines/oil/hydro 1.0, nuclear 0.15, biomass 0.90.

Heat supply (2026-07-30, DEA Technology Data v0017 datasheet row "Secondary
regulation (of full load, per minute)"):
	- Gas/oil/H2 boilers, electrode & direct-electric boilers, heat pumps,
	  biomass heat-only boilers, solar thermal: full-range regulation within
	  minutes (gas warm start 6 min; electrode 100%/min; biomass HOP 10%/min)
	  -> deliberately 0 (unconstrained). Oil/H2 boilers by analogy to gas
	  burners (engineering judgment).
	- HLI_Hardcoal 0.45 / HLI_Lignite 0.30: DEA-only reading would leave
	  these unconstrained too (coal proxy 4%/min); set to the power-side coal
	  values for cross-sector consistency. Engineering judgment.
	- HMHI_HardCoal(_CCS) / HMHI_Biomass 0.45: chain-grate/stoker/PF
	  industrial boilers 1-5%/min class, halved (practitioner-grade sources).

Steel / industry (2026-07-30):
	- BF-BOF (+CCS, Bio) 0.05: integrated-works modulation on an hourly basis
	  "not realistic"; output cuts happen via lowered tapping ratio / banking
	  on day scale (Feta et al. 2018, Tata Steel IJmuiden; Nippon Steel 2020).
	  0.05 encodes "day-scale rescheduling only". Engineering judgment on the
	  exact number.
	- DRI shaft furnaces (NG + H2) 0.20: measured first-order transition
	  tau ~1.27 h -> ~55% of a step realized within the hour; halved
	  (Ji et al. 2026, arXiv:2604.00379; Vogl et al. 2018).
	- Scrap-EAF 1.0: batch process, heats < 1 h, reserve-prequalified
	  (Paulus & Borggrefe 2011). Explicit 1.0 documents intent.
	- Molten oxide electrolysis 0.10: no public data; aluminium-smelter
	  analogy (~+-25%/h band), halved. Engineering judgment - revisit.

NOTE: 0 means unconstrained - the INFLEXIBLE continuous processes must carry
small nonzero values; leaving them at 0 would invert the physics.
